About Me

My name is Christina Bercasio, and I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Digital Screens at the University of Canterbury, majoring in Game Arts.

Gaming has been an interest of mine for as long as I can remember, and it is what led me to this field. What draws me in is not simply the act of playing, but the fact that a game constitutes an entire world — one built by hand and pure imagination.

Studying Game Arts at the University of Canterbury has made me considerably more open in my thinking. I have come to understand that aesthetics in games are not decoration layered upon a product, but rather the language through which a game speaks: a palette of colors that conveys emotion, a silhouette that communicates character, and a layout that brings a world to life. As in level design, everything consists fundamentally of pure imagination — and it is for this reason that the creation of worlds has become my primary interest.

From the early stages of learning the fundamentals of Maya in my first year to creating visual worlds in PROD 344, I have been able to gain a breadth of experience with a range of different software — an experience that has helped me grow throughout my journey as a Game Arts student at the University of Canterbury.
